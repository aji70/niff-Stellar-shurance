# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 2024_

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our services. It also describes the choices you have regarding your data, including how to request an export or deletion of your personal data.

## Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly to us, such as when you create an account, connect a wallet, or contact support. This may include:

- Account identifiers and authentication credentials.
- Wallet addresses and associated on-chain activity.
- Usage data, such as pages visited and features used.
- Communications you send to us.

## How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Provide, maintain, and improve our services.
- Authenticate you and secure your account.
- Respond to your requests and provide support.
- Comply with legal obligations.

## Cookies and Analytics

We may use cookies and similar technologies to operate the service and, where enabled, to understand how the service is used. When analytics are used, we present a consent banner that lets you accept or decline non-essential cookies before they are set. You can change your choice at any time.

## Your Rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have the right to:

- **Access** the personal data we hold about you.
- **Export** your personal data in a portable format.
- **Delete** your personal data.
- **Object to or restrict** certain processing.

Signed-in users can exercise these rights from this page using the request forms below.

## On-Chain Data Cannot Be Deleted

Some of your activity is recorded on public blockchains. Blockchain data is immutable and permanent by design. **We cannot delete, alter, or remove on-chain data**, including wallet addresses, transaction history, and any information written to a blockchain. A deletion request will remove the personal data we control off-chain, but it will not and cannot affect data that already exists on-chain.

## Data Retention

We retain personal data for as long as necessary to provide our services and to comply with legal obligations. When you request deletion, we remove or anonymize the personal data we control, except where we are required to retain it by law.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

## Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us through our support channels.
